FAQ

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I expect a reply?

We typically respond within 24–48 hours during business days. If you have an urgent technical issue, include [URGENT] in the subject line of your message.

Can I suggest a topic for a future article?

Absolutely! We welcome topic suggestions. Use the contact form with the subject "Feedback / Suggestion" and tell us what you'd like to learn about.

Do you offer one-on-one mentoring or consulting?

Yes! We offer limited mentoring sessions and consulting for Python development, OOP design, and AI projects. Reach out via email with "Consulting" in the subject line to discuss availability and rates.

Can I contribute to the open-source code?

Definitely! All content is open-source and available on GitHub. Feel free to open issues, suggest improvements, or submit pull requests.
